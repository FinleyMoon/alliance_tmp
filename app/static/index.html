Official Alliance Tactical Rulebook

1. Mission Objective & The 3 Ways to Win

Alliance is a persistent, asynchronous social-strategy wargame played on an open coordinate matrix. A match concludes in exactly one of three ways:

  • 1. Sole Survivor (Last Player Standing): Play continues until only one living player occupies the grid.
  • 2. The Peace Accord (Diplomatic Conference): When living players assemble a conference, all participants cast ballots. Unanimous agreement yields a shared Collective Victory; dissenting players trigger the configured betrayal outcome (Instant Betrayal Victory, Grant AP & Resume Play, or Resume Play).
  • 3. Inactivity Timeout: Matches with no action or movement within the stagnation deadline resolve automatically.

2. The AP Economy & Daily Drop

Every 24 hours at the scheduled server drop hour, all living units receive +1 AP. AP never expires and can be hoarded for multi-action blitzes. When a player is eliminated, all banked AP is lost.

3. Tactical Operations

  • MANEUVER (1 AP): Shift 1 tile orthogonally (N, S, E, W). Diagonals and jumping through players are prohibited.
  • STRIKE (1 AP): Strike any enemy inside your range perimeter. Deals 1 Heart damage. Line of fire is unobstructed.
  • TRANSFER AID (1 AP): Transfer 1 AP from your balance to any ally inside your interaction range (0 fee by default).
  • UPGRADE RANGE (2 AP): Permanently expands your interaction footprint outward.

4. Interaction Range Geometry & Line of Sight

5. Fog of War & Concealed Fire

Opponents' exact Hearts and AP reserves are strictly confidential. When a player is struck by non-lethal fire, they receive a private alert that they took 1 damage, but the shooter's identity remains completely unknown. Only fatal strikes (reducing a player to 0 Hearts) reveal the killer's call-sign.

6. The Diplomatic Conference & Peace Voting

Players vote to convene a conference. When living votes reach the Trigger Threshold (default 100%), deliberations open with a timed countdown window.

  • Treaty Ratification Rule (Silence is Not Consent): Any player who abstains or fails to vote before the countdown expires is officially recorded as dissenting (refusing peace).
  • Unanimous Peace (100% Agree): All survivors win together in a Collective Victory.
  • Single or Multiple Dissenters: Resolves according to lobby rules into one of three streamlined outcomes: Instant Betrayal Victory, Grant AP & Resume Combat, or Resume Combat.
  • Default Transparency: Individual ballots remain secret throughout deliberations; aggregate counts for and against are published upon conclusion.
